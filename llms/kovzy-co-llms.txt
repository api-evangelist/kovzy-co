# Kovzy

> Kovzy is a restaurant technology company operating out of Tbilisi, Georgia and Yerevan, Armenia. It sells an all-in-one SaaS platform for restaurants and cafes built from three modules — a commission-free website builder with online ordering, a QR digital menu, and a point-of-sale system that consolidates orders from delivery aggregators, QR menus, and the restaurant's own site, with kitchen management and recipe/cost tracking.

Kovzy publishes no public API, developer portal, SDK, or API documentation as of this profile. This file describes the company surface only.

## Products

- [Website builder](https://kovzy.com/en/): Restaurant websites with online ordering and no commission on direct sales (from GEL 99/month).
- [QR menu](https://kovzy.com/en/): Digital menu customers browse and order from by scanning a QR code (from GEL 25/month).
- [POS system](https://kovzy.com/en/): Point-of-sale that merges orders from aggregators, QR menus, and the website, plus kitchen management and recipe/cost tracking (from GEL 59/month).
